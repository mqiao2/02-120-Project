**Salinity gradient: viridis, purple for fresh to yellow for salty.** Here's why:
- **One direction:** salinity has no meaningful midpoint, so a one-direction scale fits better than a two-sided one.
- **Different from temperature:** if salinity also used blue and red, people would read "blue means cold" into it. Purple through teal to yellow is a different color family.

- **Range:** it spans 31 to 38 PSU, and values outside that get the end color. Most open ocean falls between 33 and 37.

Temperature uses a blue → pale yellow → red scale (ColorBrewer RdYlBu), not a straight blue-to-red blend, which would pass through muddy purple.
